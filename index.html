Projects

I turn research questions into systems people actually use.

In development at HwaljaLab

Code Weaver

CTO & Lead Developer

People design with graphs and grids. Agents write the code. Automated checks verify the result.

Code Weaver is the agent-based software development platform we're building at HwaljaLab. It brings systems for the backend, the frontend, and the database together in one app under one contract. Its repositories go by NCS (Node-Code System).

Underneath are a chat-interface spec shared by every NCS service and research on self-improving LLM agent architecture.
